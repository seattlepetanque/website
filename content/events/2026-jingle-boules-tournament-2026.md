Join Seattle Petanque Club as we head north to Civic Playfield in Edmonds for our fourth annual Jingle Boules Tournament: a select doubles tournament on Saturday December 5th. Registration is open to all interested players regardless of club affiliation.

**Details** 

Saturday, December 5th, registration opens at 8:00 am, games start at 9:00.

Teams of two, $50 per team, 24 team limit, cash payout, prize amounts tbd.

Three seeding games in the am, 55 minutes each with a tie-breaker end if necessary, all teams play at least four games (playoff format tbd).

Registration form attached, pay using Venmo (@seattlepetanqueclub) or hand Patrick Trompeter or Jeff Brann a check. Email completed registration form to [seattlepetanqueclub@gmail.com](mailto:seattlepetanqueclub@gmail.com) if paying via Venmo.

Coffee and light snacks provided in the am, bring your own lunch and beverages.

As you know, the weather in December can be mild, somewhat miserable, or completely miserable. Jingle Boules will be played in any weather condition (except lightning) so please dress accordingly. Because honestly, you would probably be playing anyway. So play with a group of people as passionate about petanque as you are. 

A special thank you to our friends at Edmonds Petanque Club, we will be using SPC’s portable courts in addition to four of the park's permanent courts for our games, leaving two courts reserved for EPC open play.
