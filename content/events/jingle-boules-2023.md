Our first annual winter Jingle Boules tournament was held in Edmonds on Saturday, December 16.

We had a great group of players enjoying some great, December weather during SPC's inaugural Jingle Boules Tournament held at Civic Park in Edmonds. Congratulations to Brad & Mark winners of the concours and to Doug & Jeri, winners of the consolante. Thanks to all players and to Maggie for taking these great photos.
