We held the annual Jouval tournament on Sunday, August 18. Fourteen teams of doubles joined us on a beautiful sunny day in South Lake Union Park. Congratulations to La and Tu, winners of the concours, and to Pete and Tahia, winners of the consolante. Thanks to everyone who participated!
