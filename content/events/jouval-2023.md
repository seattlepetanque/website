Celebrating Success at the Jouval Pétanque Tournament 2023! 🏆

On Sunday, August 6, we joined with [Petit Pierre Bakery](https://www.facebook.com/petitpierrebakery) and [Alliance Francaise Seattle](https://www.afseattle.org/) to host our annual Jouval Tournament, in memory of former Seattle Petanque Club member, Henri Jouval.

A big THANK YOU to all the wonderful participants who made this year's Pétanque Tournament at [Magnolia Summerfest](https://www.facebook.com/magnoliasummerfest) an unforgettable experience! We hope you had a blast and created lasting memories on the boules court! Let's give a big round of applause to Philippe and La, winners of the 2023 Jouval and to the second place finalists Lee and Di. 🥇🥈

A special thanks to Petit Pierre Bakery for their generous support and delicious treats that kept our energy levels high and our taste buds satisfied! Thank you to everyone for sharing the joy of Pétanque!
