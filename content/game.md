---
title: Play Pétanque
layout: game
summary: Throw boules on a gravel court with a partner of your choice (Pierre, Marcel or Odette).
---
Pick up a set of boules and choose a partner from Pierre, Marcel and Odette (the other two are your opponents). Drag sideways to turn. Press on the court where you want the boule to land, pull down to throw it higher, and release to throw; a live arc inside a pale cone shows where it may land, and orange dashes show how far it rolls. Drag back up before releasing to cancel, or hold the arrows icon (top right) to look around the thrower first. Two more icons below it show the slopes and firmness while you hold them (Read the ground) and the overhead view (let go to return to the shoulder view). Press on a boule to shoot it. When both you and your partner have boules, you choose who throws, and your partner will suggest what they would do. Get your boules closest to the jack to score.

The game needs a browser with hardware-accelerated 3D graphics (WebGL); if yours draws in software it will tell you how to turn acceleration on. The pause icon (or Esc) opens a card where you can pick Slow or Quick pace and Graphics: High, Auto (the default: it lowers the detail by itself if the game runs slowly) or Low (for older phones and computers: no shadows and less scenery).
