---
title: Play Pétanque
layout: game
summary: Throw boules against Marcel and Odette on a gravel court, with Pierre as your partner.
---
Pick up a set of boules and take on Marcel and Odette, with Pierre as your partner. Drag sideways to aim. Then swipe down and up in the lower part of the court to throw: a faster upstroke throws harder, letting go higher gives a lob, and stopping sharply before you let go adds backspin. When both you and Pierre have boules, you choose who throws, and Pierre will suggest what he would do. Prefer sliders or the keyboard? Open "Precise controls". Get your boules closest to the jack to score.
