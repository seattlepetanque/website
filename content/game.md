---
title: Play Pétanque
layout: game
summary: Throw boules against Marcel and Odette on a gravel court, with Pierre as your partner.
---
Pick up a set of boules and take on Marcel and Odette, with Pierre as your partner. Drag sideways to turn. Press on the court where you want the boule to land, pull down to throw it higher, and release to throw; a live arc inside a pale cone shows where it may land, and orange dashes show how far it rolls. Drag back up before releasing to cancel, or use the Re-aim button to turn again. Press near a boule to shoot it. When both you and Pierre have boules, you choose who throws, and Pierre will suggest what he would do. Get your boules closest to the jack to score.
